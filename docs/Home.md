# Petting Wiki

**Petting** is a Minecraft mod (mod ID `petting`) that introduces a universal taming and companion system. Any mob—passive, hostile, or boss, vanilla or modded—can be tamed with a *golden treat*. Tamed pets follow your lead, fight according to tactical *collars*, carry equipment in their own inventories, can be ridden across land, air, and sea, and can respawn at a bound bed or cushion.

Welcome to the official **Petting Wiki**. Here you will find clear and comprehensive documentation covering all features, recipes, tactical modes, riding controls, and configuration settings.

## Feature overview

| Feature | Summary | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Universal Taming** | Right-click a mob with the matching golden treat. Hostile mobs must first be weakened (30% health by default). | [Taming](Taming) |
| **Nine Golden Treats** | Treats target categories through data-driven tags, with automatic fallbacks for untagged mobs. | [Golden Treats](Golden-Treats) |
| **Sixteen Dyed Collars** | Collars store individualized AI tactics, follow distances, and optional respawn points. | [Collars](Collars) |
| **Five Combat Modes** | Passive, Defensive, Aggressive, Wander, and Guard. | [Combat Modes](Combat-Modes) |
| **Pet Inventory GUI** | Armor, hand items, collar, saddle, and chest slots depending on the mob's anatomy. | [Pet Inventory](Pet-Inventory) |
| **Universal Riding** | Full steering on land, in flight, and underwater with signature ridden attacks and rider Water Breathing. | [Riding](Riding) |
| **Collar Respawn** | Bind a collar to a bed or cushion so your pet resurrects there when taking fatal damage. | [Collar Respawn](Collar-Respawn) |
| **Golden Ender Dragon** | Infuse a Dragon Egg with a Golden Star, summon it at the End Exit Portal, and ride a dragon that never destroys terrain. | [Golden Dragon Egg](Golden-Dragon-Egg) |
| **JEI Integration** | Dedicated "Pet Taming" category displaying which treats tame which mobs. | [Compatibility](Compatibility) |
